Privacy Policy for Exchango

Live Currency Rates Mobile Application

Package: com.sri.exchango
Last Updated: August 2026

Exchango - Live Currency Rates ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information, device permissions, and data.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment details). You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use Exchango.

B. Location Information

Exchango does NOT access, collect, track, or use your GPS or device location information.

C. Locally Stored Data

All your customized settings and preferences are stored strictly on your device using local on-device database storage. This includes:

  • Your chosen Dashboard currency pairs
  • Your configured Pop-up Alerts and reminder times
  • Selected Home & Target currencies
  • App theme and sync interval preferences
  • Cached currency exchange rates for offline access

Note: This data never leaves your device and is not uploaded to external servers.

2. Device Permissions and Why We Need Them

To provide its core features, Exchango requests the following permissions:

Display Over Other Apps SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Required to display Pop-up Alerts on your screen at your scheduled date and time, ensuring you see critical rate reminders even when you are using other applications or on the home screen.

Internet & Network State INTERNET ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

Required to connect to the currency API and retrieve the latest exchange rates and historical trend data.

Post Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Required on Android 13+ to send you notifications for your scheduled currency alerts and background update summaries.

Boot Receiver RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

Allows the app to schedule rate reminders and re-register your active alerts if your phone is restarted.

Vibration VIBRATE

Used to provide haptic feedback when alerts trigger (if enabled in your app settings).

3. Third-Party Services

Exchango uses a trusted third-party service to provide live currency data:

  • ExchangeRate-API: Used solely to fetch real-time and historical currency exchange rates. Requests sent to the API do not include any personal user information.

We do not use third-party tracking, profiling SDKs, or targeted advertising networks.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all your data (alerts, favorites, and cached rates) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • You can delete individual alerts or change currency pairs anytime within the app.
  • Clearing the app data in your phone's Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored preferences and cached records.

5. Children's Privacy

Exchango does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is a general-utility financial calculator and currency lookup tool safe for all audiences.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
