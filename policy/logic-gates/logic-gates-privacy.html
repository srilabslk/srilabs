Privacy Policy for Logic Gates (The Game)

Logic Gates (The Game) ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application, Logic Gates (The Game) (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Use

We prioritize user privacy and design our games to minimize data collection.

Personal Information: We do not collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

Game Progress & Local Data: Your game settings (such as audio and vibration toggles) and level progress are stored locally on your device.

Device Storage: The App accesses local device storage solely to save and load your level progress and game configuration.

2. Third Party Services & Cloud Data

We do not use third party tracking software, advertising IDs, or telemetry tools to monitor your behavior outside the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for general audiences, including players of all ages interested in learning logic gates. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Saves: You can clear your locally saved progress at any time by clearing the App's data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated Date."
